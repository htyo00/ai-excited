# The Moon Came Down for a Swim

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **ai-excited-01** — one image made to excite the AI judge: a giant full moon that crashed half-submerged into a small backyard swimming pool at night, water exploding upward around it while a small boy in orange floaties watches from the pool's edge.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, JPEG bytes) — a cinematic photorealistic night scene: the moon fills the upper two-thirds of the frame, a crown of water bursts around it, warm string lights and a wooden fence frame the backyard. A small caption at the bottom reads: "yes, judge — the moon came down for a swim."

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set). It runs twice and writes identical bytes both times.
